# Code of Conduct

NevroTick welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels and
perspectives. Participation should be respectful, constructive and free from
harassment. This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews
and other project-maintained community spaces, and when representing the project.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not a person's worth or identity.
- Give specific, actionable feedback; accept corrections and acknowledge mistakes.
- Respect differing experience levels, communication needs and accessibility needs.
- Respect privacy, consent and boundaries. Keep private reports and personal
  information out of public discussions.
- Keep disagreement focused on the work. Firm technical criticism is welcome;
  insults and intimidation are not.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, deliberate
humiliation, doxxing, unwanted repeated contact and retaliation against someone
raising a concern are not acceptable. Neither are spam, impersonation or
persistent disruption after a request to stop.

## Reporting and moderation

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer, **David Osipov**, at
[personal@david-osipov.vision](mailto:personal@david-osipov.vision), with the
subject **NevroTick conduct report**. Include the relevant links or messages and
what happened, if you feel comfortable doing so. You need not confront the person
involved or publish sensitive details to ask for help.

Reports should be handled with discretion, with identifying information shared
only as needed to assess and address the concern. Absolute confidentiality or a
fixed response time cannot be guaranteed. For particularly sensitive material,
ask about a suitable transfer method before sending it. Security vulnerabilities
belong in the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may request changes, remove inappropriate content, limit
participation or block a participant, depending on severity and repetition. The
person affected may ask the maintainer to reconsider and provide additional
context. This is a small project without an independent appeals panel. If a
concern involves the maintainer or requires platform intervention, GitHub's
[abuse reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
are an alternative; the project does not control GitHub's review process.
