# NTP wire source provenance

This is NevroTick's S1.1a inert fixed-header decoder and S1.1b bounded outer
framing owner. The selected
source context is Chrony **4.9**, commit
`8df4f1263e206585e6c4e61352d042c2dd12916a`, particularly
[`ntp.h`](https://github.com/mlichvar/chrony/blob/8df4f1263e206585e6c4e61352d042c2dd12916a/ntp.h)
for the protocol field layout, and
[`ntp_ext.c`](https://github.com/mlichvar/chrony/blob/8df4f1263e206585e6c4e61352d042c2dd12916a/ntp_ext.c)
for extension-prefix and bounded-length parsing context. The Rust code retains that provenance; no
clean-room or language-translation licence exemption is claimed. The repository's
[source ledger](../../tasks/s0.1-chrony-ledger.json) records the retrieved file's
identity and the bounded source investigation.

The header decoder copies one exact-size header and projects raw protocol fields.
The S1.1b owner admits a complete fixed header and an immutable borrowed chain of
opaque outer extensions, under an explicitly selected no-legacy-MAC grammar.
It does not port Chrony's sample/statistical admission, server/symmetric modes,
legacy-MAC discrimination, NTS implementation, era pivot, networking or Unix adapters.
Those omissions preserve this slice's explicit bounds and are not claims of
compatibility with the complete upstream client. Independently specified fixture
facts come from the NTP format and the reviewed S1.1 specifications, not output
generated by this decoder.

The retained `ntp_ext.c` source has SHA-256
`c47ee6d4b78263da319d26bceb68dccf78a93f5be5a94d561e153b68af37c2cb`.
Its `NEF_ParseSingleField` checks the generic four-byte envelope, declared
network-order length, word alignment and available span. That generic envelope
also supports other contexts; it is not the complete outer no-MAC grammar.
NevroTick deliberately applies the independent RFC 7822 §3 outer-field minima:
16 bytes per field, 28 bytes for a single/final field without a MAC. RFC 8915
§5.6's shorter encrypted inner fields are outside this API. No version/type
semantics or Authenticator-last requirement is borrowed from other upstream
consumers: every 16-bit type, including `0x0404`, remains opaque here. A valid
outer field may follow an Authenticator without acquiring authentication.

The explicit byte ceiling of 48..=4096 and field ceiling of 0..=64 are isolated
experimental parser/campaign caps. They are not upstream limits, canonical UDP
limits, shipped transport defaults or NTS-KE budgets. Nonaligned byte ceilings
remain meaningful. Legacy MAC/crypto-NAK trailers are not guessed from their
length; syntactically valid MAC-looking extension bytes remain ambiguous opaque
fields. These deviations and boundaries require interoperability review before
any future transport adopts this API.
