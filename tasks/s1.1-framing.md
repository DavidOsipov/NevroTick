## Scope, authority and expected result

Architecture §7.1/7.3, §6.3.1 and CHECK-6/25's bounded binary-input subset apply;
CHECK-19/20/26 apply to actual source, tests and execution admission. Raw bytes
flow through one complete checked framer into immutable borrowed opaque fields.
The first intended consumer is the new independent test harness; there is no
network, exchange, authentication, time conversion or state/effect consumer yet.

Implement `frame_no_legacy_mac_datagram(&[u8], FrameLimits)` in the existing
dependency-free `no_std`, unsafe-forbidden `ntp-wire` crate. The named entry
point selects an explicit outer grammar: a fixed 48-byte header, then zero or
more extension fields, with no legacy MAC/crypto-NAK trailer. Do not guess a MAC
from remaining bytes or identify a framing success as authenticated NTS.
MAC-looking bytes can also be syntactically valid extensions; the framer parses
the whole tail as extensions and makes no claim to distinguish that ambiguity.

Independent protocol truth comes from
[RFC 7822 §3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7822.html#section-3): total field
length is network-order u16, includes its four-byte prefix/padding, is word
aligned, is at least 16 octets, and a single/final field without a MAC is at least
28. Bare 48-byte headers remain valid inert frames. Unknown types are preserved
as opaque fields; recognized-type policy belongs to a later semantic consumer.
[RFC 8915 §5.6](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8915.html#section-5.6) separately
allows shorter encrypted inner fields; no inner-field or crypto API is enabled
here. NTS ordering, cardinality, AEAD contents and authenticated boundaries stay
unqualified; valid outer fields may follow an NTS Authenticator without being
authenticated, so the framer must not manufacture protection labels.
Type `0x0404` is opaque and need not be last at this structural boundary.

## Limits and unavoidable contracts

`FrameLimits::new(max_datagram_bytes, max_extension_fields)` has private fields
and rejects byte budgets outside **48..=4096** or field budgets outside
**0..=64**. No `Default`, unchecked constructor, mutable fields or settings DTO
is introduced. These are explicit **experimental parser ceilings**, chosen to
bound this isolated implementation and campaign; they are neither canonical
UDP limits nor shipped network defaults. They are unrelated to NTS-KE budgets.
Nonaligned byte ceilings are meaningful and permitted. Future interoperability
qualification must review these ceilings before a transport adopts them.

Reject byte-budget violations before header/tail traversal; reject the next
field once the count budget is exhausted. Return a fixed redacted error with no
partial output. The public result is constructed only after the complete tail
validates. All truncation/length arithmetic is checked before slicing; successful
loop iterations consume at least 16 bytes. No allocation, I/O or retained global
state is permitted. Parsing work is bounded by input bytes and at most 64 fields.

`RawDatagram` privately retains an owned `RawHeader`, the immutable borrowed
fully checked tail and its count. It exposes a header reference, count and a
fresh bounded `ExtensionFields` iterator. Private iterator construction preserves
the checked tail invariant, allowing infallible indexing/splitting without
revalidating unchanged bytes or swallowing errors. Each `RawExtension` privately
borrows one complete field; its type, complete bytes and body are read-only.
The body includes field-specific padding; generic framing cannot identify it.
The input borrow prevents safe mutation while any view remains live. Metadata
is constant-size; no packet/cookie-bearing type derives payload-revealing Debug.
