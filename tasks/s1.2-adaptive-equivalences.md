## Checked integer facts

Write candidate fitted/auxiliary counts as `r,a`, with `L=r+a`; selection fitted
count/start and decision start/count as `q,s,d,c`. Genuine terminal construction
gives `q>=3`, `N=s+q=d+c`, and `d=max(0,N-2*q)`. Selection and its same-fit
statistics have equal frame and count. The fit owner preserves the exact input
frame on constant and general paths; statistics consume that same line/suffix.

Write intact report configured/effective minimums as `M,e`. The factory requests
`e=min(M,original_current)`, and the selector guarantees
`e<=q<=original_current`; therefore `e=min(M,q)`. A genuine minimum fallback
also guarantees `q=e`.

Raw provenance is `O,m`; after unchanged checked subtraction the validator uses
`E=min(M,O-m)` when its configured-minimum equality holds. Successful unchanged
count/window checks give `r=q`, `s+r=O`, `s>=m`. Thus `O-m>=q`.

All additions in the identities above concern genuine indices/counts <=64 and
candidate combined count <=64. Raw `O,m` may be arbitrary `usize`: failed checked
subtraction rejects identically before the changed chain, and checked additions
and saturating subtraction retain their actual integer semantics. This uses no
real-number replacement of floating-point operations.
