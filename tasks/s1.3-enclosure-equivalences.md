## Rejected complement equivalence and candidate separation

`crates/time-core/src/projection_enclosure.rs:275:62: replace || with && in Fraction::complement` is **not equivalent** on the method's complete checked-fraction domain. Its documented error contract rejects a fraction below one half, even though `add_fractions` currently calls it only for a selected fraction at least one half.

A genuine `Fraction::new(1,2)?` represents one quarter. Its comparison with one half is `Ordering::Less`. Original guard: `true || false` returns `Err(FixtureEnclosureError::Invariant)`. Mutated guard: `true && false` is false, so checked denominator 4 minus numerator 1 produces `Fraction::new(3,2)`, a successful three-quarter result. No checked projection/interval is forged. This concrete error/return difference refutes an E disposition regardless of public-path reachability.

The old private tests checked complement rejection for `(0,0)` and `(1,1041)`. Under the mutant, the former still fails downstream proper-fraction construction and the latter still fails the mutated guard. They do not distinguish the positive-below-half, shift-at-most-53 quadrant. Root has authored `(1,2)` in that existing rejection loop; its presence was inspected, but this document claims no baseline GREEN, mutant RED/kill or new candidate outcome from it. The unchanged original production guard already implements the rejection contract.
