## Boundary and proof method

Requirements are S1.3/CHECK-7/8 and CHECK-25, with the [mutation-equivalence boundary method](../.agents/skills/testing/references/mutation-equivalence.md). A helper's own input, output, exact-error, effect and lifecycle contract controls the argument.

- [Checked interval/batch owners](../crates/time-core/src/selection.rs) require ordered closed i64 intervals, nonzero unique labels, complete immutable occupied prefixes and batch capacity at most sixteen.
- `EndpointKeys` owns a sorted distinct declared prefix, `1<=keys.len<=32`, constructed by `unique_endpoints`. Initialized array padding is outside that prefix and need not be sorted.
- `CellDepths` owns a completed alternating point/open-span prefix. `depth_cells` is its sole completed producer; `append_depth` mutates construction-phase scratch. Its fields describe the key count used by its own producer.
- Separately completed keys and cells can have independent origins. No same-batch or same-origin precondition is inferred from the current caller. The new `maximum_components` entry guard independently checks their prefix-shape relationship before any endpoint read.
- `common_witness` accepts a checked batch plus an arbitrary raw `(i64,i64)` component and arbitrary raw `usize maximum`. Reversed components, zero, `usize::MAX`, empty batches and endpoint extrema remain in its checked/rejected input domain.

Only the exact single mutations below are proposed. A distinct mutation that corrupts a producer cannot be composed with one of these faults to manufacture a multi-fault counterexample. No direct field forgery, unfinished scratch or unsafe bypass is used to narrow or challenge a completed carrier.
