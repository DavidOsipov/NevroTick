The reviewed first fixture is pure and inert: a batch of 0..=16 intervals with distinct, nonzero caller-asserted labels, exact i64 integer endpoints, and **closed intervals on a continuous real axis**. These synthetic labels are not authenticated source identities; the axis is not a production clock frame. Define global maximum depth D and compute `D > floor(n/2)` independently of an explicitly checked minimum in 1..=16. Empty, insufficient population, no majority, insufficient agreement, multiple maximum components and unique witnessed component have the reviewed priority. If there is one maximum component, return its left endpoint as common witness and its complete sorted supporters and intersection; after the majority and minimum gates, multiple components produce an unsupported *fixture* ambiguity with no chosen witness. This does not settle product tie handling, no-majority behavior or source availability.
